## Failure boundary

For this internal slice, a failure with confirmed PhaseB rollback leaves the durable SHIPPING/IN_PROGRESS intent. A COMMIT/rollback indeterminate error is passed through unchanged, retains the genuine capability and proves neither rollback nor current durable state; an eventual retry must first observe the original current operation X lock and replay terminal success/failure or wait for the original transaction to settle, before any possible business execution. There is no catch-and-commit business failure transition, compensation, artificial FAILED or null→zero inference. Two small private probes validate success/conservation/replay and exact late audit rollback followed by same-capability retry after confirmed rollback; the rollback case compares complete local/Inventory/Sales facts, movements, operation journals, history/audit and snapshot rows captured after PhaseA.
